Brand story

Comfort food with a little Patna café electricity.

Zaika House is built around the food people actually crave after class, work, shopping or a long family day — creamy pasta, spicy sandwiches, loaded burgers, cold coffee, and sharing plates that make the table louder.

The space feels modern, but the taste stays familiar: Indian flavours, café classics, quick service and a table that never feels too formal.

“Not fine dining. Not fast food. Just a warm modern place where everyday cravings feel special.”
